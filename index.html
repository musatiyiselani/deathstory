The Guy Called Death

by Musa Tiyiselani

Long ago, in a village far far away, there lived a young delicate wealthy man who had the riches that could buy an entire continent. The village was ancient and well known for its horrors at night, the most terrifying stories came from that place, known as Gurallionnees - pronounced 'g-ooh-ral-ian'.

His, Boris Williams', riches made him arrogant, he cared much more about wealth than any other thing that exists, his house had almost everything one could possibly desire - his wealth gave him freedom from blame. His parents knew of this, but could not assist him to be better. He even had a theory that money is everything and without it there is no life. It was always his mindset that separated him from other individuals. He made a vow that his money is his savior. During his adulthood, he faced serious life threats and managed to overcome them with his money. However, he still never understood the real reason behind all that, the mind of his kept on worshiping wealth.

At his 80's, an old woman passed by his house, she saw him and waved, "The words I shall leave with thee, The ruler of all is greater than any kind of gold thou mayest possess." And somehow the lady vanished. Legend has it that she is one who spread rumors about the guy known as Mallory, Death in disguise, some believed it was a myth, others swore she claims that when he visits, it is the end of time, yet no one could prove it. "I will never believe in nonsense, no such thing exists" Boris once said to the woman, hence the warning. An uncomfortable threat she left him with, though he was unshaken for his arrogance had grown older than he was.

✦ ✦ ✦

One day he was woken up by the knock on his door, calm as always he opened the door and saw an old man with a giant book, one heavy book that was almost the size of a suitcase. He looked famished and ancient. The two men exchanged greetings and the famished looking man started, "My name is Death, and the book I carry with me all day hath the names of thee. I was sent by the ruler of all to collect thee. Thy time hath come to an end, for thou needest to come with me." Boris was perplexed, but he felt it was not a lie, he thought for a second and said, "Wait - I am coming". Boris prepared a very delicious and most expensive meal for Mr Death, and gave him a really powerful wine that could put one to sleep for hours. "Take this before we leave", Boris said to Death.

Death accepted the food and drank the wine, as expected Death fell into a deep sleep. And Boris came up with an idea, he took Death's book and saw it was indeed true, for his neighbour who had died a week before was on that book and it showed that accurately so. He took a magical eraser and pen, so he could swap his name with that of the person who shall be the last to die - he was trying to cheat death. After a few hours, Death woke up, and started showing gratitude. "I really appreciate this man, you know all my time I have never chosen this job, God gave me and people are afraid of me for it. I have never been treated like that before. All I get from people is tears, ruthless words and all the negative things they could possibly say or do to me, but you are different, truly one of a kind --"

"so today, I am doing this just for you, going against the rules of God because of your kindness." The old man said to Boris, Boris was filled with pride, and the old man continued, "Instead of me following the order of God, I will give you more days to live, I will start from the last person to die and page back until your name so you can be the last man to die". Against all odds, Boris happened to appear again on the last date of humanity, because of the 'swap'. Boris was almost lucky, but alas death still had him with or without his wealth.

The question is, was it fate (designed by God) or a mistake made by man?
